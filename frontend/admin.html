Actor Types

Custom role labels that define who handles each step in a workflow.

How actor types work

Actor types are institution-defined labels (e.g. "Triage Nurse", "Paediatric Doctor") that map to a system role — staff by default, but you can choose any role. A Staff CSV row whose role matches a registered label inherits that system role on login. The workflow engine routes tasks to the right inbox by actor type.
